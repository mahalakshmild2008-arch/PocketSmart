# PocketSmart AI – User Guide

## Step 1 – Open the Application

Open the PocketSmart AI application in a web browser.

## Step 2 – Add Income

1. Open the Add Income section.
2. Enter the income amount.
3. Select the income source.
4. Click Add Income.

The income will be displayed on the dashboard.

## Step 3 – Add Expense

1. Open the Add Expense section.
2. Enter the expense amount.
3. Select the expense category.
4. Enter a description.
5. Click Add Expense.

The expense will appear in Recent Transactions.

## Step 4 – Set Monthly Budget

1. Open the Set Monthly Budget section.
2. Enter the budget amount.
3. Click Set Budget.

The monthly budget will appear on the dashboard.

## Step 5 – View Dashboard

The dashboard displays:

- Total Income
- Total Expenses
- Remaining Balance
- Monthly Budget

## Step 6 – View Transactions

The Recent Transactions section displays the recorded income and expense details.

## Step 7 – View Recommendation

After adding expenses and setting a budget, the recommendation section displays a recommendation based on the amount spent compared with the budget.

## Conclusion

PocketSmart AI helps users track income, expenses and monthly budgets through a simple interface.
